Research Highlights

My research focuses on observable signatures of curved spacetime through photon propagation, polarization evolution, and timing diagnostics near compact objects.

Kerr Polarization Transport

Parallel transport of polarization vectors along null geodesics in Kerr spacetime, with applications to X-ray polarimetry and black-hole spin diagnostics.

Time-Delay Asymmetry

Mirror-paired arrival-time asymmetries in Kerr spacetime as a timing signature of frame dragging, spin, and relativistic propagation effects.

Neutron-Star Polarization

Geometric GR baselines for polarization transport in neutron-star spacetimes, motivated by IXPE-era compact-object observations.

Publications & Preprints

Selected research papers and preprints in general relativistic radiation transport, polarization, and compact-object timing.

Kerr Polarization Transport: Accuracy and Performance in General Relativistic Light Propagation

Published in The Astrophysical Journal. This work develops and validates relativistic polarization transport methods in Kerr spacetime for modeling observable X-ray polarization signatures from accreting black holes.

A New Timing Signature of Black Hole Spin: Time-Delay Asymmetry in Kerr Spacetime

This work introduces relativistic time-delay maps and mirror-paired asymmetry diagnostics in Kerr spacetime as a possible observable signature of black-hole spin, reverberation structure, and frame dragging.
