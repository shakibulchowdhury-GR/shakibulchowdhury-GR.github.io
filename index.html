Astrophysics & General Relativity

Graduate student, City College of New York

I work on high-mass X-ray binaries and GR light-bending with applications to polarization (IXPE context) and black-hole spin diagnostics. Current focus: Cyg X-1 disk polarization, first-order Kerr extensions to analytic transport, and validation against geodesic baselines.
