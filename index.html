Current Research

Kerr Polarization Transport

Parallel transport of polarization vectors along null geodesics in Kerr spacetime, with applications to X-ray polarimetry and black-hole spin diagnostics.

Time-Delay Asymmetry

Mirror-paired arrival-time asymmetries in Kerr spacetime as a possible timing signature of frame dragging and black-hole spin.

Neutron-Star Polarization

Geometric GR baselines for polarization transport in neutron-star spacetimes motivated by IXPE-era observations.

Publications & Preprints

Kerr Polarization Transport: Accuracy and Performance in General Relativistic Light Propagation

Published in The Astrophysical Journal. This work develops and validates relativistic polarization transport methods in Kerr spacetime for modeling observable X-ray polarization signatures from accreting black holes.

A New Timing Signature of Black Hole Spin: Time-Delay Asymmetry in Kerr Spacetime

Development of relativistic time-delay maps and asymmetry diagnostics in Kerr spacetime to identify observable timing signatures associated with black-hole spin, reverberation structure, and frame dragging.
