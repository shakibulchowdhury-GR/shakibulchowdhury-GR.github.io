Cyg X-1 Polarization (IXPE × NuSTAR)

Modeling polarization degree (PD) and angle (PA) vs. inner radius and spin. I use fast analytic light-bending with first-order Kerr corrections and validate against a geodesic-tracing baseline. The goal is observable-level accuracy with near real-time speed.
